Parameters

Tests localized road blockages across each neighbourhood and finds where one blocked road area would cut the most mapped buildings off from a major road.

Click two points on the map: each end joins the nearest existing road junction or road end (within 400 m).

Map key

One blocked area cuts off 30+ mapped buildings
One blocked area cuts off 1–29 mapped buildings
None of the sampled blockages cuts anyone off
Not assessed (edge of road data)
Brighter red streets = more buildings could be cut off. Only neighbourhoods with 30+ mapped buildings are shown.

Blocked area: a circle 100 m across, tested every 50 m along the roads. The blockage is a hypothetical input — this tool does not predict hazards, traffic or evacuation times.

Flood parameters

This is not a flood prediction.

You choose the river level. This shows road access under that level; it does not change the vulnerability classification.

Supplied river level: 8.36 m gauge height

Gauge heights at Saint John River at Fredericton (station 01AK003, CGVD28). The elevation model uses CGVD2013; the conversion is shown under the slider.

Fire parameters

This tool does not predict fire spread.

You supply the affected area; the map shows road access under it. The vulnerability classification is not changed.

Supplied hypothetical affected area: radius 500 m

Click the map to place the centre. The radius is your input, not a predicted fire extent.
