Parameters

Tests localized road blockages across each neighbourhood.

Test blockage radius: 50 m (scan radius)

The scan tests a fixed 50 m radius (100 m across). Any other radius is your own test and is not comparable with the scan finding.

Click two points on the map: each end joins the nearest existing road junction or road end (within 400 m).

Method / assumptions

Blocked area: a circle 100 m across, tested every 50 m along the roads. The blockage is a hypothetical input; this tool does not predict hazards, traffic or evacuation times.

Only neighbourhoods with 30+ mapped buildings are shown. Brighter red streets = more mapped buildings could be cut off. Counts use the higher of the OpenStreetMap and Microsoft footprint estimates, never their sum.

Flood parameters

This is not a flood prediction.

Supplied river level: 8.36 m gauge height
Method / assumptions

You choose the river level; the map shows road access under it. The vulnerability classification is not changed.

Gauge heights at Saint John River at Fredericton (station 01AK003, CGVD28). The elevation model uses CGVD2013; the conversion is shown under the slider. Full assumptions are listed with the results.

Fire parameters

This tool does not predict fire spread.

Supplied hypothetical affected area: radius 500 m

Click the map to place the centre, or drag the centre to move it. The radius is your input, not a predicted fire extent.

Method / assumptions

You supply the affected area; the map shows road access under it. No wind, weather, fire behaviour, traffic or evacuation time is modelled. The vulnerability classification is not changed.

The mapped 2023 perimeter is the retrospective NRCan CWFIS NBAC burned-area outline.

3D view: building heights are illustrative (one fixed height for every mapped building).
